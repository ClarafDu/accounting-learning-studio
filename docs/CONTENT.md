## Material treatment

- Original slide text, images, administrative notices, contact information and source PDFs are excluded from the public site.
- Repeated slide builds are consolidated; image-only examples of statements and disclosures were visually inspected.
- Examples distinguish recognition, measurement, presentation and cash timing. Each question has four choice-specific rationales and a conceptual hint.
- Introductory cycle/closing, selected standard boundaries and other additions are identified as supplementary. Results from a few original questions are not labelled comprehensive mastery.
- Supplementary reading: financial-report workbook and the supplied Evergrande revenue-recognition reading. AI class notes are not an independent answer authority.
- Original numerical examples use explicit simplifying assumptions; differences caused by rounding are distinguished from accounting logic. Inconsistent numbers in supplementary reading are not used as graded answers.
- Lecture 7 uses EBT/profit before tax for tax examples rather than treating EBIT as interchangeable. Deferred-tax reconciliations distinguish period changes from closing balances. EPS is distinguished from dividends or investment return.
- Recognition of DTA, warranty obligations, over-time revenue and capitalized expenditure is scoped to the stated assumptions; wording does not imply universal treatment across jurisdictions.
